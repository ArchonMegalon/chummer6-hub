## Reference Standard

- Apple Human Interface Guidelines: clarity, deference, depth, touch-safe controls, and direct task hierarchy (https://developer.apple.com/design/human-interface-guidelines/)
- Material Design 3 Foundations: deliberate color, typography, motion, layout, and component affordance systems (https://m3.material.io/foundations)
- Microsoft Fluent 2: coherent design tokens, elevation, spacing, and interaction states across platforms (https://fluent2.microsoft.design/)
- IBM Carbon Design System: 2x-grid discipline, accessible structure, purposeful density, and production-ready consistency (https://carbondesignsystem.com/)
- Atlassian Design System: clear product language, predictable navigation, humane density, and resilient component behavior (https://atlassian.design/)
- Shopify Polaris: commerce-grade task clarity, action hierarchy, content discipline, and usable empty/loading/error states (https://polaris.shopify.com/)
- GOV.UK Service Manual: plain-language service journeys where the next action and user obligation are never ambiguous (https://www.gov.uk/service-manual)
- WCAG 2.2: visible focus, legible contrast, input readability, and touch target discipline (https://www.w3.org/TR/WCAG22/)
- Nielsen Norman Group Usability Heuristics: status visibility, recognition over recall, consistency, and clear task feedback (https://www.nngroup.com/articles/ten-usability-heuristics/)

## Exit Principles

- `five_second_first_impression`: A new visitor must know what Chummer is, which build to use, and where help lives in one glance. Source posture: Apple HIG clarity plus Nielsen status visibility.
- `editorial_identity`: Typography, color, depth, spacing, and surface rhythm must be named systems, not incidental page styling. Source posture: Material/Fluent/Carbon design-token discipline.
- `premium_depth_without_noise`: Atmosphere may create depth, but it cannot compete with install, play, status, or recovery decisions. Source posture: Apple deference plus Fluent elevation.
- `one_route_one_job`: Each public route needs one visible job, one primary next action, and supporting choices that do not blur the job. Source posture: GOV.UK service design plus Shopify action hierarchy.
- `accessibility_as_finish`: Contrast, focus, form controls, touch targets, reduced motion, and mobile layout are release criteria, not cleanup. Source posture: WCAG 2.2 plus Carbon production consistency.
- `quiet_public_language`: Public pages must read like a product, not like a proof harness, provider adapter, internal roadmap, or operator console. Source posture: Atlassian content discipline plus HIG clarity.
