# Public Legal, IP, and Sourcebook Content Policy

Chummer is a fan-facing Shadowrun product and must stay explicit about boundaries.

## Public rules

- public submissions may describe a need, but may not publish raw copyrighted sourcebook text
- generated artifacts may not imply official endorsement
- trademark wording must keep the product in fan-project posture
- package, feedback, and Karma Forge flows must reject or redact raw sourcebook payloads by default
- provenance, moderation, and retention must exist before any public republication of user-provided text
