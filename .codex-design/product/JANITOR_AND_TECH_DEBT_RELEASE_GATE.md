# Janitor and Technical-Debt Release Gate

Janitor work is release work.

## Blocking findings

- public provider or LTD name leakage
- stale or weak route proof counted as success
- public acquisition wording that bypasses `chummer.run`
- missing first-party privacy, retention, or source-safe submission posture
- central canon drifting behind the Hub mirror or public route truth
- secrets, tokens, or PII in generated proof

## Exit bar

No P0 or P1 janitor finding may remain open for preview or flagship release decisions.
