# ANARCHY

## What is ready now

Anarchy is now a shipped first-party rules-light lane.

Chummer treats Anarchy as a dedicated profile for mobile play, Black Ledger consequence, dispatch reading, and explainable export without claiming full dense-book completeness.

## Live routes

* `/anarchy`
* `/play/anarchy`
* `/ledger/anarchy`
* `/anarchy/export/runner.json`
* `/anarchy/receipts/explain.json`
* `/anarchy/receipts/runtime.json`

## What it does

* keeps a dedicated rules-light profile instead of flattening Anarchy into SR5 or SR6
* reads the same Black Ledger dispatch truth through an Anarchy lens
* exports a portable first-party runner packet with provenance attached

## Boundary

Anarchy does not claim:

* full dense-book completeness
* sourcebook prose reproduction
* separate world truth outside Black Ledger receipts

It is a shipped rules-light lane, not a promise of exhaustive book transcription.
