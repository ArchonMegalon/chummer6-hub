# CREATOR OS

## The problem

Creator output too often falls apart across drafts, approval, publication, and campaign return. The public shelf is visible, but the actual truth about what was reviewed, published, and fed back into play gets buried in disconnected tools.

## What it does

CREATOR OS is Chummer's governed publication lane.

It lets a creator or operator:

* discover public-safe publications on first-party rails
* keep publication review and publication truth on signed-in Chummer routes
* tie publication state back to campaign-return summaries instead of letting output die on a shelf
* inspect public publication detail without exposing private review state
* keep provider tools, asset hosts, and promotion lanes as helpers instead of truth owners

The public route is live at `https://chummer.run/creator`.
The named signed-in lane is live at `https://chummer.run/account/creator`.
Those lanes currently carry governed publication discovery, signed-in publication detail, publication status, and campaign-return follow-through without pretending the full long-range creator roadmap is done.

## Hard boundary

* not provider-dashboard-owned publication truth
* not public exposure of draft review state
* not an asset shelf with no campaign return
* not direct publication authority granted to external render or promotion tools

## What is ready now

Chummer can now prove the core Creator OS loop:

1. public publication discovery, public publication detail, and signed-in publication detail all point at the same governed publication spine,
2. publication review, publish state, and campaign-return posture stay on first-party rails,
3. public detail remains inspectable without leaking private review state,
4. external creator tools can assist but do not outrank Chummer-owned publication receipts.

The broader creator roadmap is still larger than this shipped slice, but the governed publication network itself is no longer only a horizon claim.
