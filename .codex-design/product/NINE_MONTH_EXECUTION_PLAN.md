# Nine month execution plan

## Purpose

This is the April through December 2026 product-governor operating overlay.

It sequences the active successor wave in `NEXT_20_BIG_WINS_AFTER_POST_AUDIT_CLOSEOUT_GUIDE.md`
and the correction layer in `CAMPAIGN_OS_GAP_AND_CHANGE_GUIDE.md` into one monthly execution spine.

It does not replace `NEXT_20_BIG_WINS_AFTER_POST_AUDIT_CLOSEOUT_REGISTRY.yaml`,
`PROGRAM_MILESTONES.yaml`, `METRICS_AND_SLOS.yaml`, or `PRODUCT_HEALTH_SCORECARD.yaml`.

## Operating rules

1. Cut breadth before golden-journey proof, support closure honesty, or install/update coherence.
2. A month does not count as green if its cited journey evidence is warning or blocked without a bounded governor note.
3. If a repo cannot finish a whole month, ship the thinnest vertical slice that preserves that month's proof contract.
4. Hub, Core, UI, Mobile, Registry, Media Factory, Fleet, and EA keep owned boundaries explicit even when one month needs all of them.

## April 2026 - Golden journey operating proof

Primary objective: turn the four lived journeys into release-control evidence instead of canon-only confidence.
Source milestones: `1`, `17`, `18`, `20`
Primary repos: `fleet`, `chummer6-hub`, `chummer6-ui`, `chummer6-mobile`, `chummer6-hub-registry`

- Support closure, install trust, and public trust surfaces cite registry and install truth.
- The weekly pulse names the active checkpoint and the next bounded corrective slice.

## May 2026 - Hub bounded contexts and retention discipline

Primary objective: keep Hub powerful without letting campaign, support, public, and assistant seams collapse into one smart blob.
Source gaps: Hub super-repo risk, privacy and retention drift
Primary repos: `chummer6-hub`, `fleet`, `executive-assistant`, `chummer6-design`
Exit:
- Accounts and Community, Campaign Spine, Control and Support, Public Guide/Home/Downloads, and Orchestration/Assistant adapters have explicit APIs, projections, and retention rules.
- Support, assistant, survey, claim/install, and provider-trace redaction rules are executable enough to survive audit and release pressure.

Primary objective: close the year with a product that can justify promotion or freeze using lived evidence instead of hand-maintained hope.

- The support assistant can verify fix availability against install and channel truth before asking for confirmation.
- Public trust, onboarding, and the weekly pulse can justify launch, hold, or reroute decisions with measured adoption and closure health.

## End-of-month review rule

Every month closes with the same three questions:

1. Which golden journey is measurably safer now?
2. Which trust claim became more honest or more boring?
3. Which repo boundary stayed explicit while the product became more coherent?
